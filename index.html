Fat loss roadmap
The mathsAt 80kg / ~20% BF you carry ~16kg fat. Target ~12% BF = ~74–76kg. Need to lose ~4–6kg fat while keeping muscle.
Timeline0.5kg/week = 8–12 weeks — well within November. ~500 kcal daily deficit gets you there. Don't go faster — you'll sacrifice muscle.
Non-negotiables160g protein/day. Strength train 2–3×/week. Sleep 7–9 hours. Consistency beats perfection.
Injury modifications
Achilles tendonitisNo box jumps, jump rope, or sprinting on hard surfaces. Run on grass or treadmill. Do eccentric heel drops daily (3×15 slow negatives off a step). Build run volume ≤10%/week.
Left wrist painSwap barbell bench for dumbbells or cables. Use wrist wraps for pressing. Pull-ups and rows are fine. No loaded wrist curls or flat-palm push-ups under load.
